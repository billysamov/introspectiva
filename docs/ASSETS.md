## Hero: prompt utilizado

Modo: tool integrado, no CLI ni API. Sin imágenes de entrada. Fondo opaco.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: sophisticated editorial image for a premium Spanish digital design and development studio website hero, horizontal 1536x1024.
Primary request: a single large sculptural ribbon forming an open circular loop with a folded directional arrow-like end, a striking physical abstract object in a dark studio.
Scene/backdrop: near-black seamless #080b10 studio background and black ground, completely empty.
Subject: oversized sculptural folded ribbon, electric cobalt blue glossy lacquer on the outer surface, brushed aluminum on selected inner surfaces, elegant geometry, refined industrial product design.
Style/medium: ultra-high quality photorealistic 3D render, art-directed contemporary editorial design.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, sculpture occupies center-right with generous clean negative space on the left, entire sculpture visible; dramatic close three-quarter perspective; not centered symmetrically.
Lighting/mood: crisp cobalt rim light, controlled large softbox reflection on metal, subtle realistic contact shadow, premium and confident.
Color palette: vivid cobalt #2458ff, silver aluminum, near-black.
Materials/textures: convincingly lacquered surface and finely brushed metal with sophisticated reflections.
Constraints: NO typography, NO letters, NO numbers, NO interfaces, NO screens, NO logos, NO watermark. One main sculptural form only, clean background, no additional floating decorations.
```
